# Roster

## What does randomization do to this game?

Roster is not a game. It is a selector slot. It holds one `Unlock: <slot name>`
item for every other slot in the multiworld. A few of those are handed out at the
start; the rest are shuffled into the item pool like any other progression item.

Until a slot's unlock has been found, none of that slot's checks are considered
reachable and its goal cannot be completed. The seed is over when every selected
game is done.

## What is the goal?

Roster's own goal is holding every unlock. The goal the humans care about is
every game slot goaling on the server.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

All of them. Every `Unlock: <slot name>` item can be anywhere in the multiworld.

## What does another world's item look like in Roster?

Roster has one location per gated slot, `Started: <slot name>`, sent by the
Roster client the first time someone joins that slot.
